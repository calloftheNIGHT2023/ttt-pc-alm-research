## What is established

- A previously verified rational witness shows a genuine smooth shared-parameter stationary trap: multiplier accumulation leaves it at sweep 7, reaches support feasibility at sweep 62, and reduces exact uniform-query risk from 0.1879342658 to 0.1214341867. This is conditional same-state evidence, not cold-start or arbitrary-regression superiority.
- The earlier frozen 512-task study has a candidate MSE of 0.0561558958 versus 0.0719445434 for the strongest tested prior-feature ridge mean and 0.0937241409 for a meta-trained shallow control. It did not establish independent corrected superiority over strong optimizer or non-multiplier credit controls.
- New protocol 365 removes the entire ALM mother trajectory. On 32 exposed tasks, direct necessary-language inference produces predictions bitwise identical to mother-plus-all-pool on all 32 tasks. Both MSEs are 0.0504878776, with mean complete times 0.634304 and 1.262842 seconds respectively. The fixed research primary has MSE 0.0514310967 and 1.327700 seconds. Adam240 has 0.0512499136 and 0.730626 seconds. These are one interleaved timing per task, not repeated-timing inference.

The direct baseline is global structured inference, not a closed-form regression head and not a newly renamed PC-ALM method. It is important because equality of residual/zero-credit extensions that share an ALM mother cannot alone exclude the mother's contribution. This new ablation does so for this numerical readout on these small tasks, without contradicting the local escape proof.
